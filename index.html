Flight Info For the Swedes 🇸🇪

JFK Airport, NY (Closer)

  1. Icelandair flies from Stockholm Arlanda to JFK (connection at Reykjavik). Delta flies directly.
  2. Take the AirTrain directly from JFK to Jamaica Station.
  3. Take the LIRR train from Jamaica to Syosset.
  4. The hotel is a 7-minute drive from Syosset station.

Newark Airport, NJ (Farther)

  1. SAS flies from Stockholm Arlanda to Newark directly, but this airport is farther from the venue.
  2. Take the AirTrain directly from the airport to Newark Liberty International Airport Station.
  3. Take the NJ Transit train from Newark Liberty International Airport Station to New York Penn Station (do not get off at Newark Penn Station in New Jersey).
  4. Take the LIRR train from New York Penn Station to Syosset.
  5. The hotel is a 7-minute drive from Syosset station.
Travel Note For the Swedes 🇸🇪

ESTA — Visa Waiver

Swedish citizens traveling to the US for tourism for up to 90 days must obtain an ESTA before boarding. Applications cost $40.27, are usually approved within 6 hours, and are valid for 2 years.

Using Google Drive for photos (recommended for multi-device/browser access):

Quick method:
1. Upload images to Google Drive folder
2. Right-click each image → Share → Change to "Anyone with the link"
3. Copy the share link. It will look like: https://drive.google.com/file/d/FILE_ID_HERE/view?usp=sharing
4. Find the FILE_ID (long string between /d/ and /view) and create this URL: https://drive.google.com/uc?export=view&id=FILE_ID_HERE
5. Edit index.html with a text editor, find the old image URLs, replace with your Google Drive URL, save, and re-upload

Example: If your share link is https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ABC2DEF3GHI/view?usp=sharing, use: https://drive.google.com/uc?export=view&id=1ABC2DEF3GHI
