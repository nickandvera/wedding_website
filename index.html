Flight Info For the Swedes 🇸🇪

JFK Airport, NY

Icelandair flies from Stockholm Arlanda to JFK (connection at Reykjavik). Delta flies directly.


Newark Airport, NJ

SAS flies from Stockholm Arlanda to Newark directly.

Getting from Airport to Venue

From JFK Airport

  1. Take the AirTrain directly from JFK to Jamaica Station.
  2. Take the LIRR train from Jamaica to Syosset.
  3. The hotel is a 7-minute drive from Syosset station.

From Newark Airport

  1. Take the AirTrain directly from the airport to Newark Liberty International Airport Station.
  2. Take the NJ Transit train from Newark Liberty International Airport Station to New York Penn Station (do not get off at Newark Penn Station in New Jersey).
  3. Take the LIRR train from New York Penn Station to Syosset.
  4. The hotel is a 7-minute drive from Syosset station.
Travel Planning Tip 🇸🇪

Arriving Early for Travel Buffer

If you are traveling from abroad, we recommend arriving 1–2 days early to account for travel time and jetlag.

Travel Note For the Swedes 🇸🇪

ESTA — Visa Waiver

Swedish citizens traveling to the US for tourism for up to 90 days must obtain an ESTA before boarding. Applications are usually approved within one day, and are valid for 2 years.
